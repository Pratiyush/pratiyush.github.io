Bingo

Host a 75-ball bingo night from your phone, let friends join with a six-character code and play on their own card, or print unique cards as an A4 PDF. For Android, iPhone and iPad.

How to play

  • Host: tap Host game and read the code aloud. Call balls one at a time; the app reads each one out.
  • Players: tap Join game and enter the code. Everyone gets a different card, saved under My cards.
  • Tap numbers as they are called. A full row, column or diagonal is bingo, and the host sees who claimed first.
  • Paper cards: Generate makes unique cards to print; the host checks a printed card by its ticket number.

Privacy

No account, no names, no sign-up. Game rooms hold an anonymous ID and a ticket number. Ads come from Google AdMob. Full policy: privacy policy (English and German).
