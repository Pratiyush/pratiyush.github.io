Summary

Aeroplane Control Master is a game for Android phones and tablets, and it plays fully offline. While you play, it shows one ad banner at the bottom of the screen. After a crash you may choose to watch a rewarded video to continue that shift once. The one-time purchase “Pro: remove all ads” removes the banner and makes the continue free.

Ads come from Google AdMob, which collects data as described in section 2. Signing in to Google Play Games, for leaderboards, achievements and a cloud backup of your progress, is optional. We, the developer, run no servers and use no analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own. Apart from the order records Google Play keeps for every purchase (section 3), we receive no personal data from the game. Section 10 shows how to delete your data.

1. Data stored on your device

The game keeps these in its private storage on your device:

  • your settings (language, sound, vibration, path warnings, whether you finished the tutorial),
  • your best scores, lifetime statistics (such as shifts played, aircraft landed, time in control), your daily missions, and the badges and ranks you have earned, with the day you earned them and which ones the game has already shown you,
  • a paused shift, so you can continue it later,
  • whether your Google Play account owns Pro, so Pro also works offline,
  • your ad consent choices, stored by Google’s consent tool (section 2).

None of this is sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its storage, and “Reset best scores” in Settings clears your scores at any time.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

While you play, the game shows one banner at the bottom of the screen. After a crash it may offer a rewarded video: you choose whether to watch it, and if you do, your shift continues once. The ads are served by Google’s Mobile Ads SDK. When the game uses it, the SDK collects the following and shares it with Google:

  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location,
  • device identifiers: the Android advertising ID, the app set ID and, where applicable, identifiers of accounts signed in on the device,
  • how you use the app and its ads, such as app launches, taps and video views,
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use.

Google uses this data to show and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud. It is encrypted in transit. On devices that support them, the SDK may also use Android’s privacy-preserving advertising APIs (Privacy Sandbox), which you control in your device’s privacy settings.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): before any ad is requested, Google’s consent form lets you accept, choose purposes and ad partners, or refuse. If you refuse, Google may still show limited ads that are not personalised and do not use your advertising ID. You can change your choice at any time under Settings › Privacy options in the game.

Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (on most phones Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads). How Google uses information from apps that show its ads: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Offline the game requests no ads. A rewarded video loaded earlier may still play, and Google may receive the related data once you are online again.

If you own Pro, the game requests no ads at all and does not start the ads SDK or its consent check (its code still ships inside the app, as it does for everyone).

3. Purchases (Google Play)

Pro (“Remove ads”) is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play under Google’s terms. We never see your payment details. The game only asks Google Play whether your account owns Pro, and remembers the answer on your device. As the seller we receive the order records Google Play gives every developer (such as order number, date, item, price, tax, and the buyer’s country, region and postal code); we use them only for accounting and tax.

4. Google Play Games (optional)

You do not need to sign in: without it the whole game works, only leaderboards, achievements and the cloud backup are missing. If Google Play Games is set to sign you in automatically, it does so when the game starts; otherwise you sign in only if you tap “Sign in with Google Play Games” in Settings. Once you are signed in, Google Play Games Services links the game to your gamer profile (player ID, gamer name and avatar), and the game sends Google:

  • your scores from normal and Daily Sky shifts, for the two leaderboards,
  • the achievements you unlock,
  • one cloud save with your best scores, lifetime statistics, badges and ranks (with their dates), which of them the game has already shown you, and whether you finished the tutorial.

Your settings and your paused shift are not uploaded. Other players can see your gamer name, scores and achievements as your Play Games profile visibility allows (Everyone, Friends only or Only you).

The game includes Google’s Play Games library, which starts when the game starts and, according to Google, sends Google technical diagnostics and usage information about the library itself, to keep it stable and improve it (https://developer.android.com/games/pgs/data-collection). All of this is encrypted in transit and stored by Google. You can delete this game’s Play Games data, or your whole Play Games profile, at https://play.google.com/games/profile or in your Google Account (https://myaccount.google.com). To stop the game from using Play Games, disconnect it in your Play Games settings.

5. What the game does not do

It contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own, you have no account with us, and we do not sell or rent personal data or build profiles of you. Personalised ads are Google’s (section 2), and in the EEA, UK and Switzerland only with your consent. The game cannot access your precise location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

If you install the game from Google Play, Google may collect crash reports and installation statistics from your device under its own privacy policy and your device settings. We see only aggregated, anonymised reports.

6. Permissions

The game asks for no permission that Android would show you: no location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or notifications. In the background it uses internet and network-state access (ads, purchases, Play Games), the advertising ID (ads) and Google Play billing (Pro), plus technical permissions that Google’s libraries add for background work and Android’s ad-services APIs. Vibration uses the system’s built-in haptic feedback, which needs no permission.

7. Backups

If you have device backup turned on, Android may include the data from section 1 in the backup to your own Google account. That backup belongs to you; we cannot see it. You can turn it off in your device’s backup settings.

8. If you contact us

If you email us, we use your address and message only to answer you, and delete them 12 months after the matter is closed, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

10. How to delete your data

Aeroplane Control Master has no accounts and keeps no player data on any server of ours. This is how you delete what exists, and what stays:

  • On your device (best scores, statistics, badges and ranks, missions, settings, a paused shift): “Reset best scores” in Settings clears your scores at once; uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android’s app settings, deletes all of it. We keep no copy.
  • Google Play Games (scores, achievements, the cloud save): delete this game’s data in the settings of the Google Play Games app or at https://play.google.com/games/profile, or through your Google Account (https://myaccount.google.com). Google then deletes it under its own rules.
  • Advertising: reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (on most phones Settings › Google › Ads), and change or withdraw your ad consent under Settings › Privacy options in the game. Data Google already holds for ads follows Google’s retention rules (section 11).

What stays: the order record of a Pro purchase, which Google Play and we must keep for tax (section 11), and Google Play’s note that your account owns Pro, so you can restore it.

We hold nothing about you on any server. Email pratiyush1@gmail.com and you get these steps within 30 days.

11. Retention

Google keeps the ads data (section 2) and the Play Games data (section 4) under its own retention rules (https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention). We keep order records as long as tax law requires (in Germany up to 10 years). Emails: see section 8.
